bkr comes with a few small widgets. They work in reading systems that support scripting, and they fall back to plain, readable text everywhere else. Each one is ordinary markup with a data-bkr attribute.

A quick quiz

Which file holds the title and the order of the chapters?

  1. content/01_introduction.html
  2. config/book.yml
  3. assets/css/book.css

Right: config/book.yml describes the book, and content/ holds the chapters themselves.

One step at a time

How to make a book with bkr:

  1. Write your chapters in content/.
  2. Fill in config/book.yml.
  3. Run book build.
  4. Open build/ and enjoy your EPUB!
